# Callouts: visual identity

Written before any composition HTML, as the Hyperframes skill requires. Source: video edit SOP v0.4, "Look" tab. The ALC companies and Andrew share one visual spine, and it is the default look: white first, dark ink, one blue accent, Montserrat and Inter, clean lines, restrained motion.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | transparent | the render is an overlay, so no ground colour |
| card | #FFFFFF | the callout card |
| ink | #111111 | headline |
| body | #3B4252 | label line |
| accent | #0066FF | the type tag only |
| hairline | #D9E1EE | card border, so a white card reads on a white slide canvas |

## Type

- Headline: Montserrat 800, tracking -0.03em, 42 to 80 px by length (never under 42 px).
- Label: Inter 400, 30 px.
- Type tag: Montserrat 700, 20 px, uppercase, tracking 0.12em, accent blue.
- Quote headline: Montserrat 600 italic.

Deviation from the Hyperframes typography guide, logged: the guide bans Inter and warns against pairing two sans-serifs. The SOP look is Montserrat plus Inter across Andrew's and Wolf & Eagle's work, and the SOP says the kit wins. One voice (headline) and one that recedes (label), weight contrast 800 against 400.

## Layout

Canvas 1060 x 260, card anchored bottom-left. The compositor places it at (40, 800) in mode C (inside the caption band, from y 790) and at (40, 740) in mode B (inside x 40 to 1100, y 700 to 1000, never over the face).

## Motion

Restraint, no bounce. Card eases up 14 px and fades in over 0.35 s (power3.out). It fades out over 0.3 s before its out time. No ambient decoration: a held card is still.

## Copy rules (SOP stage 11)

Headline 7 words or fewer, a stat 4 or fewer. Wording from resume v0.7 or the speaker's own words. Hold at least 3 s, or 1 s plus 0.3 s a word, whichever is longer.

## Do not

No gradients, no left-edge accent stripes, no dark cards, no emoji, no second accent colour.
